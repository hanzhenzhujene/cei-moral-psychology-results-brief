# Research lead brief

## Decision

Publish the measurement story and the canonical audit. Hold the poster numerical comparisons until their source package is restored.

The current materials support a strong methods message: task design, administration, scorer behavior, and judge choice materially shape the observed result. They do not support a model moral leaderboard, a ranked profile, an exact paper-replication claim, or a human-validity claim.

## Three decisions for the research lead

1. **Choose the release type.** A methods and measurement release is supportable now. A numerical poster-results release is not.
2. **Assign evidence recovery.** One owner should restore the minimum poster packet and resolve the missing evidence pin and seal.
3. **Approve the validity plan.** Gate M and a cross-family judge panel should be designed before adding more model rows.

## Highest-value next action

Recover one complete poster result from item input through final chart. Use it as the reference implementation for the other three posters. This tests the entire evidence chain and will reveal whether the legacy numbers survive the current instrument.
